Frequently asked questions

What kind of SaaS is Muse Partner?

Muse Partner is intentionally generic: it's a flexible workspace for organizing clients, projects, and internal work. You can position it however you like in your marketing copy.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Muse Partner runs in the browser. Your team can log in from any modern device with an internet connection.

Can I connect it to my existing tools?

Yes. Muse Partner is designed to sit alongside your existing stack. Use simple exports today, and integrations/webhooks as they're released.

Is there a free trial?

You can start with a time‑limited trial before choosing a plan. Just mention this on your live marketing site or signup flow.
